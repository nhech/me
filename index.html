ABOUT

How I work.

I start from the failure modes. Before a service ships I want to know what it does when it is slow, when it is called twice, and when the network drops mid-transaction — because that is exactly where money and digital assets go missing.

I choose storage per access pattern, not per habit: a relational DB for users and billing, NoSQL for sessions and message history, Redis for the hot path. Service boundaries follow data ownership. Auth, rate limiting and routing live at the gateway so individual services stay small enough to reason about.

I have led backend teams at Appota and Vtech. In review I look at correctness and blast radius first and style last, and I would rather operate a small surface well than ship a large one I cannot debug at 3am.
